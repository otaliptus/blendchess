    HOW TO PLAY

    Normal chess rules, one twist: you only see your own pieces. Everything else is fog.

    • The umpire tells both players when someone moves, when a piece is captured (and on which square), and when a move attempt was illegal. Nobody hears which move.
    • Check: only the player in check is told, along with the line it comes from: file, rank, diagonal or knight.
    • Illegal tries: pieces may run into things you can't see. Try again. In hard mode an illegal try costs your turn, unless you're in check.
    • Hints: dots show squares that might be legal. Dashed corners are pawn captures you can try.
    • Game over lifts the fog: replay every move and export the PGN.
    • Spectators see the fog too, unless the host turned on party mode.

    KEYS: arrows move the cursor, ENTER picks or drops a piece, ESC cancels, M toggles sound.
